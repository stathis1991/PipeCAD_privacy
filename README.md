# Privacy Policy for PipeCAD

**Last updated: May 2026**

## Overview

PipeCAD is a 3D pipe planning application developed by Efstathios Christofas. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles your information.

## Information We Collect

**PipeCAD does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.**

All data you enter in the app — including:
- Client names, phone numbers, VAT numbers, and email addresses
- Pipe assembly designs and fittings
- Project configurations

— is stored **locally on your device only** using iOS UserDefaults and local storage. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to any server.

## Data Storage

- All project data is stored locally on your iPhone or iPad
- Client information entered in Settings is stored in your device's local storage
- No data is synced to cloud services
- No data is shared with third parties

## Network Usage

PipeCAD may optionally connect to a local PocketBase server on your local network for advanced project management features. No data is transmitted over the internet.

## PDF and CSV Exports

When you export a Bill of Materials as PDF or CSV, the file is saved to your device or shared via iOS Share Sheet. Sharing is entirely under your control.

## Analytics and Tracking

PipeCAD does not use any analytics services, crash reporting tools, or advertising SDKs. The app contains no tracking code.

## Third-Party Services

PipeCAD does not integrate with any third-party services that collect user data.

## Children's Privacy

PipeCAD is a professional tool not directed at children under 13.
